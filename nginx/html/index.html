Kotlin FHIR Engine codelab

Your lab has three parts. Open the Editor and the App in separate tabs, in the same browser, and keep both open. Open the FHIR server when a step asks you to check it.

EditorYour code, the guide and the build log. It asks for the password your lab printed. AppThe app you are building. It reloads automatically after each build. FHIR serverThe patients on the server your app syncs with. It takes a minute or two to start.

Scan the QR code to preview the app on your phonePoint the camera of the phone at the code. The App opens in its browser.

  1. Change a file in the Editor and save it with Ctrl+S.
  2. Watch the Build log under your code until it says BUILD OK.
  3. Switch to the App tab, which has reloaded automatically.
